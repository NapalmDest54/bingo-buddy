Bingo Buddy

For iPhone · In beta testing

Bingo Buddy keeps track of your paper bingo cards. Take a photo of your sheet (or type a card in), check the numbers it read, then tap or type each number as it's called. Every card shows how close it is to a bingo, so you don't miss one.

Your cards stay on your iPhone. There's no account to make, and the app works without a connection.
