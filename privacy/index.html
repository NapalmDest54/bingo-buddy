Privacy Policy

Bingo Buddy · Effective October 3, 2026

Bingo Buddy (the "App") is made by Christopher Capps ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the App uses, why, and the choices you have. In short: there's no account, we don't run any servers, and your cards, games and photos stay on your iPhone.

Information the App keeps on your iPhone

The App saves your cards (their numbers, names and groups), photos of your cards, the games you play and the numbers called in them, your settings, and whether you have a Bingo Buddy Plus subscription. All of this is stored on your iPhone and is not sent to us. Card photos are stored at a reduced size, without location (GPS) information.

If iCloud Backup is turned on, your iPhone includes this information in its backup, which Apple stores and protects. Scans you haven't finished are left out of backups.

How long it's kept and how to delete it

The information stays on your iPhone until you delete it. You can delete cards, groups and games in the App. Past games keep their own copy of the cards that were played until you delete those games. Deleting the App removes everything it stored on your iPhone. Copies in an existing iCloud backup are removed when that backup is deleted or replaced (iOS Settings > [your name] > iCloud > Manage Account Storage > Backups).

Camera and photos

The App uses the camera only when you choose to scan a card. It reads the numbers on your iPhone with Apple's on-device text recognition; your photos are never uploaded. If you choose a photo instead, the App receives only the photo you pick and can't see the rest of your photo library.

Subscriptions

Bingo Buddy Plus is sold through the App Store. Apple processes the payment; we never see or store your payment details. The App asks Apple whether your subscription is active and remembers the answer on your iPhone, so it keeps working without a connection. You can manage or cancel a subscription in iOS Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions.

Beta testing

If you test a pre-release version through Apple TestFlight, Apple shares limited information with us, such as your name or email address (as entered in your invitation), device and app version, crash reports and any feedback or screenshots you choose to send. We use it only to improve the App. See TestFlight & Privacy.

Children

Bingo Buddy is for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. It doesn't collect personal information from anyone.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the CCPA), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information, or to object to its use. We don't hold personal information about you: what the App stores stays on your iPhone, where you can delete it as described above.
